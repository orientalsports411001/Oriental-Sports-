## 2. Turnkey Court Construction Portfolio (`CourtsPage.tsx`)

### 1. Pickleball Courts & Facilities
* **Surface Systems**:
  - *Multi-Layer Cushion Acrylic System*: Impact-absorbing rubber granule matrix underneath resilient color coats to reduce knee and lower back fatigue.
  - *Hard Court Sports Acrylic*: High-traction, non-glare, UV-stable surface for tournament play.
  - *Modular Interlocking Sports Tiles*: Fast-draining perforated polypropylene tiles for rooftop and multi-use community setups.
* **Regulation Standards**: USAPA and international regulation layout (20ft × 44ft playing court with non-volley kitchen zone).
* **Hardware & Fencing**: Heavy-duty 3-inch in-ground steel posts, internal brass gear winding net systems, and galvanized chainlink containment fencing.
* **Lead Time**: 3 to 4 Weeks.

### 2. Championship Tennis Courts
* **Sub-Base Engineering**: Asphalt or reinforced PCC concrete sub-base with precision 1:100 dual-plane drainage slope for rapid monsoon drying.
* **Cushioning Technology**: 8-stage acrylic system consisting of resurfacer, heavy rubber base coats, fine cushion coats, wear coats, and UV-resistant color layers.
* **Line Marking**: Sharp, textured acrylic boundary lines preventing ball skid.
* **Lead Time**: 4 to 6 Weeks.

### 3. Indoor Badminton Hall Facilities
* **Surface Options**:
  - *Sprung Teak / Hardwood Flooring*: Seasoned timber slats mounted on shock-absorbing neoprene pads, providing high energy return and zero joint stress.
  - *PVC Vinyl Sports Mats (4.5mm – 7.0mm)*: BWF-certified textured surface with embossed grip and anti-slip wear layers.
* **Lighting Systems**: Asymmetric, indirect glare-free sports fixtures (300 to 500 Lux) positioned to eliminate blinding during high overhead clears.
* **Lead Time**: 2 to 3 Weeks.

### 4. Box Cricket & 5-a-Side Football Synthetic Turfs
* **Turf Construction**: High-density 40mm – 50mm non-directional monofilament synthetic grass.
* **Infill Composition**: Calibrated silica sand base layer topped with cryogenic SBR rubber granules for realistic bounce, ball roll, and sliding safety.
* **Enclosure Architecture**: Heavy-duty galvanized tubular steel truss frame, UV-stabilized braided nylon perimeter netting, and top ceiling net to retain balls during high shots.
* **Lead Time**: 3 to 5 Weeks.

## 3. Four-Phase Engineering Workflow

```
[Phase 1: Site Audit & Laser Grading]
  ├── Soil compaction test & topography mapping
  └── Laser-leveled sub-base preparation (concrete or asphalt) with integrated drainage

[Phase 2: Base Preparation & Cushion Acrylics]
  ├── Acrylic resurfacer application to seal pores
  └── Multi-coat elastomer cushion layer application for joint shock absorption

[Phase 3: Color Coating & Precision Line Marking]
  ├── UV-stable non-glare concentrated color wear layers
  └── 100% acrylic white regulation boundary lines masking and painting

[Phase 4: Hardware, Lighting & Commissioning]
  ├── In-ground post sleeves, tournament nets, and chainlink enclosure
  └── Floodlight aiming, photometrics lux testing, and formal client handover
```
