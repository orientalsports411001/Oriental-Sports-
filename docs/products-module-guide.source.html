1Before you start

The admin panel is where the online shop is managed. Anything you change here is what customers see on orientalsports.in.

Two separate addresses

admin.orientalsports.inThe admin panel. Staff only. This is where you add, edit and remove products.
orientalsports.inThe public website. This is what customers see.

How quickly changes appear

When you save or delete a product, the public website is updated automatically. There is no separate "publish" or "sync" step to run.

  • A customer opening the site after your change sees it straight away.
  • A customer who already had the page open sees it within about a minute, or as soon as they refresh.
Please note: there is no undo. A deleted product cannot be restored from the panel — it has to be added again by hand. Read section 8 before deleting anything.

2Signing in

  1. Go to admin.orientalsports.in.
  2. Click Continue with Google.
  3. Choose the store's authorised Google account.

Only the authorised store account can get in. Any other Google account is refused, even if the sign-in itself works. If you are refused, you are using the wrong account — ask the owner which one to use.

If sign-in fails with a message about a code or storage: open admin.orientalsports.in directly in Chrome or Edge rather than from a link inside WhatsApp, Instagram or Gmail, and complete the sign-in in one go without pressing Back or refreshing.

3The Equipment Catalog screen

Open Products from the menu. This screen lists every product on the website.

The four figures at the top

FigureWhat it means
Total CatalogEvery product in the system, including hidden ones.
Published & LiveProducts customers can currently buy.
Low StockProducts at or below their warning level. Click it to see only those.
Inventory ValueSelling price multiplied by units held, added up.

Finding a product

  • Sport buttons (All, Cricket, Badminton, Tennis and so on) narrow the list to one discipline.
  • Search box — type part of the product name or brand.
  • Low Stock shows only products running out.
  • All Statuses filters by Published, Draft or Archived.

The product list

4Adding a new product

Click + New Product at the top right of the Products screen. The form has four tabs. You can move between them freely — nothing is saved until you press Save Product.

As you type, a live preview on the right shows how the product card will look to customers.

Tab 1 — Basic Info & Specs

FieldWhat to enter
Product TitleThe full name customers see, for example SG Scorer Classic Cricket Bat. Required.
URL Handle (Slug)Fills in by itself from the title. Leave it alone unless asked to change it.
Sport DisciplineThe category the product belongs to. This decides which section of the website it appears in, so choose carefully. Required.
Equipment BrandManufacturer, for example SG, Yonex, Nivia.
Specifications & NotesThe description customers read: material, size, weight, what it suits. Required, and must be a proper sentence or more.
Sizes & VariantsOnly if the product comes in sizes. Put one size per line. Leave empty if there are none — an empty box simply hides the size selector.

Tab 2 — Pricing & Stock

FieldWhat to enter
Selling Price (₹)What the customer actually pays. Required, and must be more than zero.
Original MRPOptional. Enter it only if there is a discount. It appears struck through next to the price, and the saving is worked out automatically. Leave empty if there is no discount.
Available Physical UnitsHow many you have. At zero the product shows as out of stock.
Low-Stock Warning TriggerThe level at which the panel warns you. Five is a sensible default.
Storefront VisibilityPublished, Draft or Archived. See section 7.

Tab 3 — Media

  • Drag photos in, or click to choose them from the computer.
  • Up to 5 photos per product. At least one is required.
  • Each file must be an image and under 10 MB.
  • The first photo is the main one shown in listings, so put the best one first.
  • Wait for each upload to finish before saving.

Tab 4 — Search & SEO

This controls how the product appears in Google results. You can leave it empty. If you do, the title and description are filled in automatically from what you already typed. Only fill it in if you have been given specific wording.

Saving

  1. Press Save Product at the bottom.
  2. Wait for "Product created successfully".
  3. Open the product on orientalsports.in in a new tab and check it looks right.
If it refuses to save, a red message says exactly what is missing — usually no photo, no description, no price, or no sport chosen. Fix that one item and press Save again.

5Editing an existing product

  1. Find the product using the search box or the sport buttons.
  2. Click Edit on its row.
  3. Change what you need. It is the same four tabs as when adding.
  4. Press Save Product and wait for "Product saved successfully".

Everything can be changed later — price, photos, description, stock, category. Changing the Sport Discipline moves the product to a different section of the website.

6Updating stock

There are two ways, depending on what you are doing.

  • Correcting the number — open Edit, go to Pricing & Stock, and type the right figure in Available Physical Units.
  • Recording a delivery or a loss — use Warehouse Stock Audit & Adjustment lower down the edit page. Enter how many units to add or remove and a short reason. This keeps a dated record of who changed what, which the plain figure does not.

Use the adjustment panel for stock that physically arrives or leaves. Use the plain figure only to fix a mistake.

7Hiding a product without deleting it

Most of the time you do not want to delete. Change the status instead — it can always be changed back.

StatusWhat happensUse it when
PublishedLive on the website. Customers can buy it.Normal selling.
DraftHidden from customers. Still in the panel.Still preparing it, or waiting on photos.
ArchivedHidden from customers. Kept on record.No longer sold, but you want the history.

To change it: Edit → Pricing & Stock → Storefront Visibility → Save.

8Deleting a product

Deleting removes the product from the website permanently. Use Draft or Archived instead whenever you might want it back.

  1. Find the product in the list.
  2. Click Delete on its row.
  3. A confirmation box appears. Read the product name and make sure it is the right one.
  4. Click Delete to confirm, or press Esc to cancel.

Products that customers have already ordered

If a product appears in a past order, it is archived instead of deleted, and you will see "Product archived to protect order history". This is deliberate. It disappears from the website just the same, but the old invoices and order records stay correct. Nothing has gone wrong.

If deleting fails, a red message appears and the product stays. Do not keep clicking — tell the person who looks after the website. A deletion is never reported as done unless it really was.

9If something looks wrong

What you seeWhat to do
Saved, but not on the website Check the status is Published and stock is above zero. Then refresh the website page with Ctrl + Shift + R.
In the wrong section of the site The Sport Discipline is wrong. Edit the product and change it.
Shows "out of stock" Units are at zero, or the status is Draft or Archived.
Photo did not appear It was over 10 MB, was not an image, or Save was pressed before the upload finished. Edit the product and add it again.
Panel count and website count differ The panel also counts Draft and Archived products, which customers cannot see. A difference here is normal.
A red error message Note down the exact wording and pass it on. The wording says what went wrong.

Rules worth remembering

  • Every product needs a title, description, price, sport and at least one photo.
  • Five photos maximum, each under 10 MB. The first is the main one.
  • Sizes go one per line, or leave the box empty.
  • Fill in Original MRP only when there is a genuine discount.
  • Prefer Draft over Delete. Deleting cannot be undone.
  • Always check the live website after saving.
